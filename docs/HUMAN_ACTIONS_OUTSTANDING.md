# Human Actions Outstanding Register

This register documents every constant, parameter, flag, hardware component, or operational procedure in the codebase that is **PROVISIONAL**, **UNCALIBRATED**, **UNCONFIRMED**, or **BLOCKED ON PHYSICAL HARDWARE**.

In a production agronomic system, software cannot substitute for physical calibration sheets, optical bench unmixing, soil percolation infiltrometer tests, or field phenological inspection. Leaving these parameters at their unconfirmed defaults produces systematic silent biases or runtime exceptions.

> **Scope Note (7 Sep 2026):** Autonomous irrigation actuation hardware is dropped (no solenoid valves, MOSFET drivers, pumps, or YF-S201 flow meters are procured). Actuation is advisory-only; all calibration actions for valves, pumps, and flow meters are excluded from this register. Row 12 (`WATER_LEVEL_SENSOR_PRESENT`) is retained because paddy basin depth sensing is still required for advisory irrigation prescriptions.
